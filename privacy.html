Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Pawn Breaker ("the game") is published by Pawn Breaker ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game on Android or iOS.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no online leaderboards. Your scores, campaign progress, daily streak, chosen pawn colour and sound settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). There is a small banner during play, and optional video ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game reward.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (and similar device identifiers),
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location,
  • information about your device and app (model, operating system, app version, language),
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps), and diagnostic information.

Google uses this to show ads, including personalised ads where you have agreed to them, to limit how often you see an ad, to detect fraud and to report ad performance. This data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive information that identifies you personally.

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google's privacy policy

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): on first launch you are asked whether you agree to the use of your data for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the "Ad privacy choices" link on the game's main menu.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the wording varies by device).
  • iPhone and iPad: you can control tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and personalised ads in Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.

Declining personalised ads does not remove ads; it means the ads you see are not based on your activity.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Data retention, deletion and security

We hold no personal data about players, so there is nothing for us to delete. Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept and protected under Google's own policies, linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact
