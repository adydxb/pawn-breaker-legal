Pawn Breaker

One ember pawn. An endless ivory court.

A turn-based survival game on a chessboard. Every enemy moves the way it does in chess, and red squares show where the court will strike next. Dodge, charge your strike, and break them. Five realms, five bosses and a new daily board every day.
