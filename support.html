Support

Found a bug, stuck on a realm, or have an idea?

Contact us

Send us a message. It opens a public form on GitHub (a free account is needed). Please don't include personal details. For a bug, say which device you play on and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

How do I play? Open "How to play" on the main menu. Swipe to move one square; red squares show where the court will strike next. Your strike charges as you move, and when it is full you can step into a piece to capture it.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. There are no accounts, so progress does not move between devices, and uninstalling the game removes it.

How do I change my ad choices? Tap "Ad privacy choices" on the main menu (shown where it applies), or use your device's ad settings. See the Privacy Policy.
